09 / EDIT THE SCENE

Cut on the change.
Keep the reaction.

Import the selected clips into your editor in storyboard order.

Trim

Remove the waiting. Start near the action.

Connect

Match eyelines, direction and action across cuts.

Hold

Give his choice and her response time to land.

Watch it once without sound. Can someone understand who wants whom—and what changed?
YOUR RESULT A complete picture edit, without relying on music to explain it.
10 / SOUND & EXPORT

Make the choice
audible.

Dialogue first

Keep the insult clear. Lower other sounds underneath it.

Let the room change

A murmur, the chair scrape, then quiet. The pencil stops.

Music last

Optional. Use licensed audio; let the important moment breathe.

Export → watch on your phone → share.

Use the project’s frame rate and shape. A common starting delivery: MP4/H.264, 1920 × 1080 for 16:9 or 1080 × 1920 for vertical. Check captions, voices and the final frame.
