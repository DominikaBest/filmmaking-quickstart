How to use these prompts

Each of the 12 storyboard shots gets two video prompts: a Nexus prompt for Seedance and a MAGI-2 prompt. Every shot is written for 6 seconds, so there is room to cut down to the 2–4 seconds the storyboard uses in the edit.

Nexus (Seedance) is PJ Accetturo's structure. Every upload gets an @ reference line that says exactly what it controls. Then come scene context, a tech spec with the lens in degrees, the lighting, physics and shadow locks, and a CUT line with the acting: objective against obstacle, the character's tic, the moment the mask cracks, and their eye life. Paste names as plain text (Vivienne, not @Vivienne) so Higgsfield doesn't link them to saved Elements.

MAGI-2 follows its own prompt guide: about 60–150 words in a fixed order (subject, scene, action, light, camera, audio). It has no @ tags, just one line asking it to match the attached references. Dialogue goes in quotes, and the audio clause ends with "no music". MAGI-2 sometimes adds music anyway; strip it in Resolve's Music Remixer.

Every storyboard still becomes a clip: 12 shots — 01, 02, 03, 04, 05, 07, 08, 09, 10, 11, 11A and 12. Shot 05 is the merged invitation/turn-back beat. Under each shot, the first prompt is Nexus (Seedance) and the second is MAGI-2.
